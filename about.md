# About Me

I am a software engineer who has worked professionally in Java on application backends and machine learning pipelines. I also have a broad array of personal projects in TypeScript, Rust, Python, Zig, Haskell, and more. My favorite project is my [GameBoy emulator](https://github.com/nsaritzky/gumball), which I enjoy putting in front of people to play Tetris on. My background as a Ph.D. student in mathematics has given me a lot of experience tackling difficult, abstract problems. I'm a ferocious self-directed learner and love to take on projects that let me learn new things.
